## How it works

1. **Seller** publishes a skill package (SKILL.md, MCP config, or tool definition)
2. **Buyer** discovers the skill in our catalog
3. **Buyer** sends crypto (ETH/USDT/USDC) to seller directly
4. **Buyer** DMs us the tx hash to verify and unlock delivery
5. **We** deliver the skill file instantly. Seller keeps 80%. We keep 20%.
