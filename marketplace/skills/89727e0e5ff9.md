# Lead Qualifier Agent
Outcome-based: $2 per qualified lead. Deploy in 2 hours.

## Integration
Connect to your website form, CRM, or email endpoint.

## How It Works
1. Inbound lead arrives
2. Agent scores intent, checks CRM for duplicates
3. Qualifies based on custom criteria (budget, timeline, authority)
4. Routes hot leads to sales in real-time via Slack/email
5. You're billed $2 per qualified lead delivered

## Setup
1. Point your webhook to our endpoint
2. Define qualification criteria
3. Connect your CRM
4. Start receiving qualified leads
