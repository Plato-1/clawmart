# Insurance Policy Comparison Engine

Side-by-side clause-level comparison. Save $840/year average.

## Policy Types
- Auto, Homeowners/Renters, Life (Term, Whole, Universal)
- Health (Individual, Family, Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement)

- Coverage comparison matrix (what's covered, what's not)
- Exclusion analysis: hidden exclusions vs. industry standard
- Premium benchmarking: are you overpaying vs. similar profiles?
- Policy language risk scoring: identify unusually restrictive clauses
- Renewal recommendation: stay or switch?
