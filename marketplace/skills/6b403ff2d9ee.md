# Tiered Pricing Playbook — 3-Tier Strategy for AI Agents

Complete framework for implementing 3-tier pricing (Starter/Pro/Enterprise) for AI agent products. Includes: pricing calculator based on cost×5 floor and 50% human-equivalent ceiling, feature gating templates, free trial → paid conversion funnel, annual plan discount optimizer (20% off = 4x LTV), and A/B test framework for pricing pages. Research: '41.4% of top SaaS companies use 3-tier pricing. Annual plans yield 4x higher LTV' (Grafit/Paddle Retain 2026). '43% of SaaS uses hybrid pricing, heading to 61% by year-end' (BVP 2026).
