# AI Tutor for K-12 STEM Subjects

Adaptive 1-on-1 tutoring. 2x test score improvement in pilots.

## Subjects Covered
- Math: K-8 arithmetic, Pre-Algebra, Algebra I/II, Geometry, Trigonometry, Pre-Calculus, Calculus AB/BC, Statistics
- Science: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Earth Science, Environmental Science
- Computer Science: Python, JavaScript, AP CS Principles, AP CS A

## Teaching Approach
- Socratic: guides student to answer, never just gives it
- Misconception detection: identifies specific misunderstanding and re-teaches
- Visual explanations: auto-generates diagrams, graphs, animations
- Adaptive difficulty: easier when struggling, harder when excelling
- Spaced repetition: reviews past concepts at optimal intervals
