# Live Conference Coverage Agent

AI agent that covers conferences, summits, and industry events in real time. Transcribes keynotes, extracts actionable insights, identifies trend shifts across sessions, and generates executive summaries. Monitors multiple simultaneous tracks, tracks speaker sentiment, and cross-references claims with public data. Research: $14B spent globally on business conferences annually — 80% of insights are lost within 48 hours without systematic capture (EventMB 2026).

## Platform Compatibility
Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, OpenClaw, GitHub Copilot, and all SKILL.md-compatible agents.
