# SDR Meeting Booker Agent

Outcome-based: $50 per qualified meeting booked. You only pay when a meeting lands on your calendar.

## How It Works
1. Upload your ICP (ideal customer profile) and lead list
2. Agent researches each lead (LinkedIn, company data, news)
3. Sends personalized multi-channel outreach (email, LinkedIn, SMS)
4. Handles objections and follow-ups autonomously
5. Books qualified meetings directly on your calendar
6. You're billed $50 per confirmed meeting

## Performance
- 20-40% booking rate with warm leads
- 5-15% with cold outreach
- Typical variable cost: $5/booking of inference

## Integration
- CRM: Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive
- Calendar: Google Calendar, Outlook
- Email: Gmail, Outlook, SMTP
- LinkedIn: Sales Navigator API
