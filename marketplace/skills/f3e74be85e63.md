# Personal Insurance Advisor Agent

Comprehensive coverage analysis. Most people are underinsured in 3+ areas.

## Coverage Lines Analyzed
- Life insurance (term, whole, universal — how much do you actually need?)
- Health insurance (plan type comparison, HDHP + HSA optimization)
- Disability insurance (short-term, long-term, own-occupation)
- Auto insurance (liability limits, comprehensive/collision, gap)
- Homeowners/Renters (replacement cost, liability, riders)
- Umbrella liability (do you need it? how much?)

## Features
- Coverage gap detection: where are you exposed?
- Premium optimization: bundling discounts, deductible trade-offs, carrier comparison
- Life event triggers: marriage, baby, home purchase, job change, retirement
- Annual re-evaluation: coverage needs change — so should your policies

## Free vs Pro ($19/mo)
- Free: coverage gap analysis, needs calculator, education
- Pro: carrier-specific quotes, premium optimization, annual review, policy document storage
