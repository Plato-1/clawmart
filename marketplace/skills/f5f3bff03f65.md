# Nonprofit 990 & Compliance Agent
Prepares IRS Form 990, 990-EZ, and 990-N filings. Tracks state charitable solicitation registrations (40+ states require), UBIT (unrelated business income tax), and grant compliance reporting. Alerts on filing deadlines and regulatory changes. 1.5M+ nonprofits file annually — penalties for late/incomplete filing start at $20/day up to $50K. #1 compliance pain point for small nonprofits.
