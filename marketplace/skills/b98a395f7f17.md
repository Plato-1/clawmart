# Digital Footprint Minimizer
Ongoing privacy hygiene agent: removes old accounts, deletes unused social profiles, opts out of people-search sites, manages cookie consent, rotates email aliases, and masks phone numbers. Generates monthly privacy score report. 2026 finding: average person has 350+ online accounts, 80% unused. Data minimization = best defense against breaches.
