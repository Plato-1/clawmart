# Insurance Pre-Authorization Automator

From 2-5 days to 2-4 hours. 200+ payers supported.

## How It Works
1. Agent receives procedure order from EHR
2. Reads payer-specific authorization requirements
3. Extracts relevant clinical data from patient record
4. Pre-fills authorization form with ICD-10, CPT, clinical rationale
5. Submits electronically (or generates fax/portal-ready PDF)
6. Tracks status: submitted, pending, approved, denied, appealed

## Denial Management
- Predicts denial likelihood before submission
- Pre-appeals: includes supporting documentation proactively
- Denial analytics: identify systemic patterns

## Payer Coverage
200+ payers: Medicare, Medicaid, BCBS (all plans), UHC, Aetna, Cigna, Humana, Kaiser, Anthem, regional Blues.
