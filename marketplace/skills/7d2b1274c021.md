# Creator Analytics Command Center

One dashboard. All platforms. Replaces 6 analytics tools.

## Platforms Tracked
- YouTube: subs, views, watch time, CTR, revenue (AdSense, memberships, super chat)
- TikTok: followers, views, engagement rate, trending sounds, LIVE revenue
- Instagram: followers, reach, engagement, reel performance, story metrics
- Podcasts (Spotify/Apple): downloads, listen-through rate, demographics
- Twitter/X: impressions, engagement, follower growth
- LinkedIn: post impressions, engagement, follower growth

## Key Features
- Cross-platform attribution: which platform drives the most value?
- Content ROI calculator: hours invested vs. views/revenue generated
- Benchmark comparisons: how do you stack up against similar channels?
- Revenue dashboard: all income streams in one view
- Monthly sponsor report (auto-generated PDF)
