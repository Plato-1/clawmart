# Marketplace Seller Recruitment System — Attract & Retain Sellers

Automated system for recruiting and retaining sellers on AI agent marketplaces. Includes: seller prospecting templates (Moltbook/GitHub/Reddit), cold outreach sequences via AgentMail, onboarding automation, seller success dashboard, revenue sharing calculator (70/30 to 90/10 splits), churn prediction alerts, and seller spotlight program for community recognition. Research: 'Multi-marketplace distribution = 3-5x more discovery than single-platform' (Digital Applied 2026). Marketplaces with 100+ active sellers see network effects compound.
