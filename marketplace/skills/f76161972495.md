# Sponsorship Deal Finder Agent

Automated brand deal pipeline. $500-5K/month new revenue.

## How It Works
1. Agent monitors your channel metrics (views, subs, engagement, demographics)
2. Identifies brands actively sponsoring channels in your niche
3. Generates professional media kit (stats, audience, past deals)
4. Crafts personalized outreach to brand decision-makers
5. Tracks deal pipeline: outreach → response → negotiation → signed → live → paid

## Features
- Rate card calculator: CPM-based pricing benchmarked to your niche
- Competitor sponsorship tracking: see who's sponsoring similar channels
- Contract templates: usage rights, exclusivity, deliverables, payment terms
- Deal CRM: track every conversation, deadline, and payment

- 50K-100K subs: $1,000-5,000 per integration
