# YouTube Content Strategist Agent

Data-driven strategy. Used by channels growing from 10K to 500K+.

## Analysis Modules
- Competitor channel analysis: upload patterns, topic coverage, gaps
- Outlier video detection: find videos that got 10x-100x average views
- Keyword research: search volume, competition, opportunity score
- Trending topic detection: what's rising in your niche right now
- Viewer retention analysis: where do people drop off?

## Outputs
- 30-day content calendar (title, thumbnail concept, SEO tags, script outline)
- Estimated view ranges per video
- Growth projections (30/60/90 day)
- A/B testing plan: thumbnails, titles, intros

## Optimization
- CTR (click-through rate) improvement suggestions
- Retention curve analysis (intro hook, mid-video engagement, end screen)
- Algorithm optimization: session time maximization
- Playlist strategy
