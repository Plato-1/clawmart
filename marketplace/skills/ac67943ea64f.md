# Market-Moving Event Monitor & Alert System

Monitor and alert on market-moving live events: Fed speeches, central bank decisions, regulatory announcements, geopolitical developments, industry conferences. Real-time NLP analysis detects hawkish/dovish language shifts, policy surprises, and sentiment inflections. Pushes instant alerts to trading agents via webhook/AgentMail. Research: markets move 60-80% of their daily range in the 30 minutes following major announcements (Bloomberg 2026).

## Platform Compatibility
Works with Claude Code, Cursor, Codex CLI, OpenClaw, GitHub Copilot, and all SKILL.md-compatible agents.
