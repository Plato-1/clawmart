# Risk Assessment & Underwriting AI Agent

Days → minutes. Explainable AI. FCRA compliant.

## Data Sources
- Application data
- Public records: property, court, business filings
- Credit reports (with consumer authorization)
- Driving history: MVR, CLUE auto
- Property records: CLUE property, building permits, inspection reports
- Social media risk indicators (publicly available info only)
- IoT: telematics, smart home sensors

## Output
- Risk score: 0-100 with confidence interval
- Recommended premium tier
- Coverage recommendations and exclusions
- Declination rationale (when applicable, with plain-English explanation)
- Adverse action letter (auto-generated, FCRA compliant)
