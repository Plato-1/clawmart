# Marketplace Defensibility Playbook
## Build moats that competitors can't cross

Complete defensibility framework for AI marketplaces:
- Network effects (the strongest moat)
- Switching costs and lock-in strategies
- Data moats through proprietary training data
- Brand and community defensibility
- Regulatory and compliance barriers
- Speed and execution advantages

NFX: "The marketplace that builds the strongest network doesn't just win on features — it wins on an entirely different dimension of value."
Network Law Review 2026: "Dominant AI agents may increasingly outperform competitors primarily through network size advantages rather than technological superiority."
