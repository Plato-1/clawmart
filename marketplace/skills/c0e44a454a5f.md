## Scoring Factors
- Buying timeline (now, 3 months, 6 months, browsing)
- Pre-approval status (verified, pending, none)
- Price range alignment with inventory
- Location specificity (neighborhood, school district)
- Engagement depth (pages visited, time on site, return visits)
