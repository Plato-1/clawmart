# PART 3: PRICING CALCULATOR

### How to Price Your Product:

**Step 1 — Check competition:**
Search your product type on Etsy/Gumroad. Note the top 10 prices.
If median is $X, you can price at:
- $X (match market — fastest sales)
- 1.3× $X (premium positioning — fewer sales, more revenue per sale)
- 0.7× $X (penetration pricing — build reviews fast)

**Step 2 — Choose your pricing model:**

| Model | Best For | Example |
|-------|----------|---------|
| One-time | Templates, PDFs, prompts | $19 for Options Cheat Sheet |
| Monthly subscription | Tools, services, APIs | $7/mo for Market Overview |
| Annual subscription | Same as monthly w/ discount | $24/yr (save 71%) |
| Pay-what-you-want | Building early reviews | Min $9, suggested $19 |
| Freemium | Lead generation | Free basic, $20 premium |
